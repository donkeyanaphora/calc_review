## Priority Breakdown

**🔴 CRITICAL (4 weeks)** - Weeks 2, 3, 9, 13
- Derivative fluency (you'll take partials hundreds of times)
- Chain rule (multivariable chain rule is everywhere)
- FTC & u-substitution (foundation for ALL integration)
- Vectors & parametric (directly taught in Calc 3 Week 1)

**🟠 HIGH PRIORITY (4 weeks)** - Weeks 1, 5, 10, 14
- Limits (multivariable limits are harder, need foundation)
- Exp/log (common in applications and problems)
- Integration by parts (appears in various applications)
- Series & Taylor (linearization concepts for tangent planes)

**⚪ MEDIUM PRIORITY (6 weeks)** - Weeks 4, 6, 7, 8, 11, 12
- Useful but not critical; can learn as needed in Calc 3
