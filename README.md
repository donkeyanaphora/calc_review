## Priority Guide

**⭐⭐⭐⭐ CRITICAL** — Cannot skip. Core material used constantly in Calc 3
- **Week 2**: Derivative fluency (you'll take partials hundreds of times)
- **Week 3**: Chain rule (multivariable chain rule is everywhere)
- **Week 9**: FTC & u-substitution (foundation for ALL integration)
- **Week 13**: Vectors & parametric (directly taught in Calc 3 Week 1)

**⭐⭐⭐ HIGH PRIORITY** — Skip only if desperate, review independently if skipped
- **Week 1**: Limits (multivariable limits are harder, need foundation)
- **Week 5**: Exp/log (common in applications and problems)
- **Week 10**: Integration by parts (appears in various applications)
- **Week 14**: Series & Taylor (linearization concepts for tangent planes)

**⭐⭐ MEDIUM PRIORITY** — Can skip if time-pressed, learn on-the-fly as needed
- **Week 4**: Implicit differentiation (useful but not universal)
- **Week 6**: Curve sketching (less critical for multivariable)
- **Week 7**: Max-min optimization (you'll relearn in multivariable context)
- **Week 8**: Related rates (motion intuition helpful but not essential)
- **Week 11**: Trig integrals (specific technique, less universal)
- **Week 12**: Partial fractions/improper integrals (nice-to-have algebra skill)

### If You Must Skip Weeks (Emergency Triage)

**10-week plan** (skip 4 weeks): Drop weeks 4, 6, 7, 8, 11, or 12 (pick 4)  
**8-week plan** (skip 6 weeks): Keep ONLY weeks 1, 2, 3, 5, 9, 10, 13, 14  
**6-week sprint** (bare minimum): Keep ONLY weeks 2, 3, 9, 10, 13, 14
